---
name: Clinic logo assets
description: Why the clinic logo has separate web and favicon image assets.
---

The source clinic logo is a square PNG with large white margins around a horizontal wordmark. Use a trimmed transparent wide version for visible header and footer placement, and a small square derivative for the favicon.

**Why:** Rendering the original square source in a fixed-height header makes the actual wordmark appear much smaller than intended.

**How to apply:** Keep the source upload unchanged, but derive UI-specific assets when adjusting logo placement or branding.
